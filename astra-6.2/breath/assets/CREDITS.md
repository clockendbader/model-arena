| Trachea, main bronchi, segmental bronchial trees, thoracic skin, ribs and cartilages, clavicles, sternum, thoracic vertebrae, diaphragm, heart chambers and coronary branches | BodyParts3D 4.0, [official part-of OBJ archive](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/20130619/partof_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip). |

The [mesh inventory](anatomy-manifest.tsv) identifies every contributing file, its anatomical name, and its destination group. The adaptations comprise torso cropping, grouping, axis conversion, scaling, GLB packaging, regenerated normals, materials and animation. Cardiac chamber geometry and coronary vessels provide context for the cardiac notch. The 4.0 source data were supplied with 99% polygon reduction. [BodyParts3D download catalogue](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html)

## Exhibit-generated assets

Distal airways, tracheal ridges, the alveolar cluster and duct, capillaries, erythrocytes, gas sprites, tissue shading, studio lighting, graph, interface icons, and breathing sound are generated by the exhibit. No external photographs, audio recordings, fonts, or HDR images are used.
