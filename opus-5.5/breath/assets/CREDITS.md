## Anatomy: BodyParts3D

- **Source:** BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan
- **Download page:** https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
- **File used:** `partof_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip` ("is-a / part-of" tree, version 4.0, OBJ, full resolution)
  plus `partof_element_parts.txt` (maps each FMA concept to its mesh elements)
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en
- **Attribution:** "BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan"

`tools/build_models.mjs` converted these meshes from millimetres into a metre-based,
Y-up scene frame, cropped and decimated them (meshoptimizer), and packed them as glTF binary:

| Output file | Contents | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| `models/skeleton.glb` | Ribs 1–12 (left and right), costal cartilages, sternum (manubrium, body, xiphoid), clavicles, vertebrae C7–L1, intervertebral discs | BodyParts3D meshes, decimated. Rib-head pivot points were computed for the breathing motion. |
| `models/organs.glb` | Trachea, right and left main bronchi, diaphragm, aorta, pulmonary trunk, superior vena cava, oesophagus | BodyParts3D meshes, decimated |
| `models/organs.glb` | **Five lung lobes** (right upper, middle, lower; left upper, lower) | **Derived.** BodyParts3D 4.0 has no lung-surface mesh. Each lobe was grown on a 2 mm voxel grid from its own real BodyParts3D bronchial and pulmonary-vessel tree elements, inside the cavity bounded by the real ribs, spine, diaphragm and mediastinal organs. The result was smoothed and meshed with surface nets. |
| `models/organs.glb` | **Heart** | **Derived.** Union of the BodyParts3D heart chambers, atrial walls and coronary vessels (FMA7088 parts), closed and re-meshed as one surface |
| `models/torso.glb` | Glass torso shell | BodyParts3D skin surface, cropped to the chest and decimated |
| `data/airways.json` | Bronchial tree, about 10,900 branches, generations 0–15 | **Derived.** Generated by space-filling volume bisection (after Kitaoka et al. 1999 and Tawhai et al. 2004) inside the derived lobes. Seeded by the real segmental bronchi centroids from BodyParts3D; radii follow Murray's law. |

## Generated in code (no external asset)

- Alveoli, capillaries, red blood cells, the alveolar duct, airflow and gas particles: procedural geometry and shaders in `src/alveolus.js` and `src/airways.js`
- Breathing sound: synthesised live with the Web Audio API (`src/audio.js`), no recordings
- Physiology values: computed from standard equations (alveolar gas equation, alveolar ventilation, Severinghaus O₂-dissociation curve) in `src/physiology.js`
